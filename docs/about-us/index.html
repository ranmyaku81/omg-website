About Oahu Medical Group

An independent infectious disease practice in Honolulu, caring for Oʻahu patients since the practice’s founding by physicians who trained and stayed here.

We diagnose, treat and prevent infections caused by bacteria, viruses and fungi — from pneumonia and cellulitis to HIV, bone and joint infections, and infections that have not responded to first-line treatment. Our seven physicians are board-certified in Infectious Diseases and hold leadership roles in infection prevention and antimicrobial stewardship at Oʻahu’s hospitals.

Most of our patients come to us from their primary care physician or from a hospital admission. We work alongside that physician: you keep your doctor, and we add the specialist piece — the diagnosis, the antibiotic plan, and the follow-up until the infection is resolved.

When treatment means weeks of IV antibiotics, our own home-infusion nurses make it possible to go home rather than stay in the hospital or travel to an infusion center.
