Refer a patient

We see referred patients for outpatient consultation and manage home IV antibiotic therapy. Urgent referrals are usually seen within a few days.

How to refer

  1. Complete the one-page referral form, or use your own.
  2. Fax it to (808) 528-5507 with the most recent progress note, relevant cultures and labs, imaging reports, and the patient’s insurance information.
  3. We call the patient to schedule and fax or send you the consult note after the visit. For urgent problems, call (808) 531-7111 and ask to speak with the physician on call.

What we see most

  • Bone and joint infections
  • Prosthetic joint infections
  • Diabetic foot infections
  • Bacteremia and endocarditis
  • Cellulitis that fails first-line therapy
  • Recurrent UTI with resistant organisms
  • HIV
  • Nontuberculous mycobacteria
  • Fungal infections
  • Fever of unknown origin
  • Pre-travel and post-travel evaluation

East Hawaiʻi patients

Dr. Thomas sees referred patients at Hilo Benioff Medical Center every other Friday afternoon, and our home-infusion nurse covers Hilo and the surrounding districts. Fax Hilo referrals to the same number and note “Hilo clinic” on the form. Hilo clinic details

Home IV antibiotics (OPAT)

For patients being discharged on IV antibiotics, our team arranges the home infusion, nursing visits, weekly labs and line care, and follows the patient to the end of therapy. Contact us before discharge so the first home dose is ready when the patient arrives.
