HIV care

Ongoing treatment for people living with HIV, new diagnoses, and PrEP for prevention.

What it is

HIV is a treatable, long-term condition. With modern medicines, usually one pill a day or an injection every two months, the virus becomes undetectable in the blood, the immune system recovers, life expectancy is close to normal, and the virus cannot be passed on to sexual partners. Our physicians have cared for people with HIV in Hawaiʻi for decades, including those newly diagnosed, those transferring care from the mainland, and those who have been on treatment for many years.

Starting or transferring care

At a first visit we review your history and any previous treatment, check your viral load, immune cell count and general health, screen for other infections, and talk through the options. Treatment starts right away, often the same day for a new diagnosis. If you are transferring care, bring your most recent labs and a list of the medicines you have taken; we can usually continue your current regimen without a gap.

Ongoing care

Once the virus is suppressed, visits are every three to six months with labs, and we handle prescription refills, prior authorizations and manufacturer assistance programs. We also look after the things that matter more as people with HIV grow older: heart health, bone health, kidney function, cancer screening, vaccinations and mental health, in coordination with your primary care physician. Our office staff are experienced with the state AIDS Drug Assistance Program and the insurance questions specific to HIV medicines.

Prevention

For people who are HIV-negative and at risk, we prescribe PrEP as daily pills or long-acting injections, with no referral needed. If you have had a possible exposure in the last 72 hours, call the office or go to an emergency department the same day for PEP.

Call us if

  • You have missed doses or run out of medicine
  • A new medicine or supplement started elsewhere, so we can check for interactions
  • Fever, rash, or a new illness that is not settling
  • You are planning a pregnancy or have become pregnant
