How we can help

All services

If you’re in the hospital

Your hospital doctor may ask us to see you. We visit daily, and then see you in our clinic after you go home.

If your doctor referred you

We call you to set up a visit in Honolulu, or in Hilo every other Friday. Bring your medication list and insurance card.

If you need IV antibiotics at home

Our nurses teach you the infusion, visit for line care and labs, and are on call day and night. Home infusion guide
