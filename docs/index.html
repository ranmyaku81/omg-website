How we care for patients

All services

In the hospital

We consult on infections at hospitals across Oʻahu — The Queen’s Medical Center, Kuakini, Straub, Pali Momi, Queen’s – West Oʻahu and Adventist Health Castle — and continue care in clinic after discharge.

In clinic

Outpatient diagnosis and treatment of complex, persistent or recurring infections, with a plan shared back to your referring physician.

At home

Home IV antibiotic therapy (OPAT) coordinated by our own nurses, so you can finish treatment at home instead of an infusion center. A nurse is on call 24 hours a day.
