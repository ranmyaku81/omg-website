Hilo clinic

Infectious disease care on the Big Island: hospital consultation and clinic visits at Hilo Benioff Medical Center, and home IV antibiotic therapy across East Hawaiʻi.

Clinic visits

Dr. Benjamin Thomas sees patients in Hilo every other Friday afternoon, in the Orthopedics office on the first floor of Hilo Benioff Medical Center. Visits are by appointment; call to find the next clinic date.

In the hospital

Dr. Thomas also sees patients admitted to Hilo Benioff Medical Center. If you are in the hospital with a serious infection, your hospital team can ask for an infectious disease consultation; he will direct testing and antibiotics, plan any IV therapy you need at home, and see you in the Hilo clinic after you go home — no trip to Honolulu needed.

Getting a clinic appointment

Ask your primary care physician or hospital team to fax a referral to (808) 528-5507 and write “Hilo clinic” on it. We call you to schedule.

If you need to be seen sooner than the next Hilo date, we can usually see you in Honolulu or arrange a telehealth visit; ask when you call.

Home IV antibiotics in East Hawaiʻi

Our home-infusion program covers Hilo and the surrounding districts. A nurse visits to teach you or a family member the infusion, checks the IV line and draws weekly labs, and the practice delivers your doses. A nurse is on call 24 hours a day.

Next clinic date

Clinic falls on alternate Fridays. Call (808) 531-7111 for the next date and to schedule.
