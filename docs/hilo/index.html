Hilo clinic

Infectious disease care on the Big Island: clinic visits every other Friday afternoon at Hilo Benioff Medical Center, and home IV antibiotic therapy across East Hawaiʻi.

Clinic visits

Dr. Benjamin Thomas sees patients in Hilo every other Friday afternoon, in the Orthopedics office on the first floor of Hilo Benioff Medical Center. Visits are by appointment; call to find the next clinic date.

Getting an appointment

Ask your primary care physician or hospital team to fax a referral to (808) 528-5507 and write “Hilo clinic” on it. We call you to schedule. Patients who were seen by Dr. Thomas in the hospital are followed up in the Hilo clinic rather than having to fly to Honolulu.

If you need to be seen sooner than the next Hilo date, we can usually see you in Honolulu or arrange a telehealth visit; ask when you call.

Home IV antibiotics in East Hawaiʻi

Our home-infusion program covers Hilo and the surrounding districts. A nurse visits to teach you or a family member the infusion, checks the IV line and draws weekly labs, and the practice delivers your doses. A nurse is on call 24 hours a day.

Next clinic date

Clinic falls on alternate Fridays. Call (808) 531-7111 for the next date and to schedule.
