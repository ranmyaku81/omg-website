Services

We follow an infection from diagnosis to cure, wherever the patient is: hospital, clinic or home.

Hospital consultation

When a hospital physician asks us to see a patient, we evaluate the infection, direct testing and antibiotic choice, and follow the patient daily until discharge. After discharge, we see the patient in clinic to confirm the infection is resolving and to adjust or stop treatment.

Outpatient consultation

Referred patients are seen in our Honolulu clinic for infections that are complex, slow to resolve, or recurring. We take a thorough history, review prior cultures and imaging, order what is missing, and send a written plan back to the referring physician.

Home IV antibiotic therapy

Outpatient parenteral antimicrobial therapy (OPAT) lets patients finish weeks of IV antibiotics at home. Our nurses teach you or a family member to run the infusion, visit for line care and labs, and are on call 24 hours a day. Doses are prepared by the practice and delivered to you.

What to expect at a first visit

  1. Your referring physician faxes us a referral with recent notes, labs and imaging. We call you to schedule — usually within a few days for urgent problems.
  2. Arrive 15 minutes early with your medication list, insurance card, and the completed new patient form if you printed it at home.
  3. The visit typically runs 45–60 minutes. You leave with a plan, any prescriptions or lab orders, and a follow-up date. Your referring physician receives the consult note.

Also offered

  • HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) — no referral needed.
  • HIV treatment and long-term care.
  • Antimicrobial stewardship and infection-prevention leadership at Oʻahu hospitals.
