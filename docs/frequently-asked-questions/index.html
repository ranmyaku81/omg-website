Common questions

Do I need a referral?

For most visits, yes — ask your primary care physician to fax a referral to (808) 528-5507. PrEP visits do not need a referral; just call.

How do I schedule, change or cancel an appointment?

Call (808) 531-7111 during office hours, or send a request online. If you have a Queen’s MyChart account you can also request changes there and a medical assistant will follow up.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance if you need to reschedule or cancel.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major plans. Call the office with your plan name and we will confirm before your visit.

Where do I park?

Enter the Hawaiki Tower lot from Piʻikoi Street and park in any stall marked “OMG” on your right. Our entrance is around the corner on Waimanu Street. See parking and directions.

How long is a first visit?

Plan on 45–60 minutes. Follow-up visits are usually 15–30 minutes.

How do I pay a bill?

Each physician bills under their own practice entity, so use the link for your physician on the Pay my bill page. The entity name appears on your statement.

Who do I call about home IV antibiotics after hours?

If you are receiving home infusion through us, a nurse is on call 24 hours a day at the number on your infusion instructions. For emergencies, call 911.
