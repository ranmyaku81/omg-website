PrEP: medicine that prevents HIV

Pre-exposure prophylaxis is a daily pill or a long-acting injection that keeps HIV from taking hold. We prescribe it without a referral.

How well it works

Taken as prescribed, PrEP reduces the risk of getting HIV from sex by about 99%, and from injection drug use by at least 74%. It is much less effective when doses are missed.

Who it is for

PrEP may be right for you if you test negative for HIV and any of the following apply:

  • You have had anal or vaginal sex in the past six months and have a partner with HIV, do not consistently use condoms, or have been diagnosed with an STI in that time.
  • You inject drugs, or share needles or other injection equipment.
  • You have been prescribed PEP (post-exposure prophylaxis) more than once, or after ongoing exposure.

You can also choose PrEP if none of these apply to you. If you have a partner with HIV and are planning a pregnancy, PrEP can protect you and your baby before, during and after pregnancy.

Starting PrEP with us

Call (808) 531-7111 and ask for a PrEP visit. No referral is needed. The first visit includes an HIV test, kidney function and other baseline labs, and a conversation about which option fits your life. Follow-up visits and HIV testing happen every two to three months while you are on PrEP.
