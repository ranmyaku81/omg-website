Parking and directions

88 Piʻikoi Street, Honolulu, HI 96814 — on the ground floor of Hawaiki Tower, at the corner of Piʻikoi and Waimanu Streets.

GPS tip. Navigation apps may send you up the Hawaiki condominium ramp. Don’t take it. For the parking entrance, search for Westley Rowe LLC, Piʻikoi Street instead, or use the map link and enter from Piʻikoi.

Parking

  1. Enter the parking lot from Piʻikoi Street. Look for the sign “Parking for Hawaiki Residents, KHON TV, Oahu Medical Group.”
  2. Immediately after entering, the patient stalls are on your right.
  3. Park in any stall with the hanging sign “Oahu Medical Group Patients” or “OMG” painted in red on the ground. Parking is free.

Walking to the clinic

  1. Walk back out of the lot to Piʻikoi Street and turn left, past the KHON2 building.
  2. At the corner, turn onto Waimanu Street. Our entrance, with the Oahu Medical Group sign, is on your left.

Drop-off

If someone is dropping you off, they can stop briefly in the ride-share stalls on Waimanu Street, right by our door.
