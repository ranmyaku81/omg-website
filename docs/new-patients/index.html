Your first visit

What to bring, what happens, and how long it takes.

Before you come

  • Photo ID and insurance card(s)
  • A list of every medication and supplement you take, with doses — or bring the bottles
  • Any antibiotics you have taken in the past few months, and whether they helped
  • The new patient form, printed and completed if you can
  • Names of your other doctors, so we can send them our note
  • A family member or friend, if you would like someone to listen with you

If your doctor referred you, they have usually already sent your records. If you were seen in a hospital, we can get those records ourselves.

What happens at the visit

Plan on about an hour. We go through the history of the infection in detail — when it started, what has been tried, what the cultures showed — and examine you. We review any imaging and labs, and order what is missing. You leave with a plan in plain language: what we think is going on, how we will treat it, how long it will take, and when we will see you again. Your referring doctor receives the same plan in writing.

Follow-up visits are shorter, usually 15 to 30 minutes.

Insurance and payment

We accept most major plans. Call the office with your plan name if you want to confirm before your visit. Each physician bills under their own practice, so your statement and online payment link will show the physician’s practice name. Pay my bill

Cancellations

Please give us 24 hours’ notice if you need to reschedule or cancel, so the time can go to another patient.

Your records and privacy

You receive our Notice of Privacy Practices at your first visit. To request copies of your records or have them sent elsewhere, fax a signed authorization to (808) 528-5507 or ask at the front desk.
